How to play

Goal: get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

  • 2-10 are worth their number, J/Q/K are 10, an Ace is 11 or 1.
  • Click chips to place a bet, then press Deal.
  • Hit takes a card. Stand keeps your total.
  • Double doubles your bet and gives exactly one more card. Only on your first two cards.
  • Over 21 is a bust and you lose.
  • The dealer must hit until reaching 17 or more.
  • Hint suggests the move that basic strategy recommends.

Payouts: win pays 1 to 1, Blackjack (Ace + 10-value card) pays 3 to 2, a push returns your bet.
